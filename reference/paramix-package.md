# paramix: Aggregate and Disaggregate Continuous Parameters for Compartmental Models

A convenient framework for aggregating and disaggregating continuously
varying parameters (for example, case fatality ratio, with age) for
proper parametrization of lower-resolution compartmental models (for
example, with broad age categories) and subsequent upscaling of model
outputs to high resolution (for example, as needed when calculating
age-sensitive measures like years-life-lost). Methods and application to
calculating years of life lost are described in Goodfellow et al. (2025)
[doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013420](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013420)
.
